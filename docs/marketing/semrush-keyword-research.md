## Paid competition: the market is open

Semrush found **no paid advertisers** in the US for any of these terms: `vision board`, `gratitude journal`, `law of attraction`, `abraham hicks`, `journal app`. Combined with the roadmap notes ("manifestation" and "vision board app" have no advertisers, "positive affirmations" is mostly Ad Grants churches), the paid side of this whole category is almost uncontested.

Two cautions. Where nobody bids, Google often shows fewer or no ad slots, so the Semrush CPC estimates are soft. And low competition does not mean cheap clicks convert; the search intent here is mostly informational.

### YouTube

Semrush's keyword databases cover Google web search only. YouTube search volume is **not** included in any of these numbers, and the Semrush connection in this session has no YouTube database. Semrush sells a separate "Keyword Analytics for YouTube" app in its App Center; it is not exposed through the MCP connection.

Two proxies from Google data say the Abraham Hicks audience is video-first:

- Roughly 4,000 searches a month put "youtube", "videos" or "utube" next to the name (abraham hicks youtube 1,300; youtube abraham hicks 480; abraham hicks on youtube 320; esther hicks youtube 320; abraham hicks videos 260; and a dozen smaller spellings).
- YouTube holds position 2 for the head term and three more top-15 slots (a video, YouTube Music podcast, the channel).

Implication: a written Abraham Hicks page on vibrationfit.com will mostly catch the "explain this to me" and "emotional scale / focus wheel" intent. The "play me a rampage" intent lives on YouTube and Spotify.

## Method

Semrush MCP reports used: `phrase_these` (batch overview with KD and intent), `phrase_fullsearch` (broad match), `phrase_related`, `phrase_questions`, `phrase_organic` (top ranking pages), `phrase_adwords` (paid advertisers), `domain_rank`. Filters: KD below 40 (45 for law of attraction, 35 for manifest), volume above 200 to 500 where the broad match was noisy. "Related" reports for journaling and law of attraction were discarded as noise (plants, law schools); broad match was used instead.
