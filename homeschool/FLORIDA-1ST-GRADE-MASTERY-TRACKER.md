# Florida 1st Grade Mastery Tracker (Parent Dashboard)

**Last verified against official standards:** August 9, 2026
**Sources:** Florida B.E.S.T. Standards (ELA + Math), NGSSS (Science), Florida State Academic Standards — Social Studies 2023
**Companion doc:** `oliver-ocean-adventures/07-FLORIDA-1ST-GRADE-ALIGNMENT.md` (legal requirements, portfolio system)

---

## How to Use This

Glance at this every 3–4 weeks and ask: *"Have we naturally covered this yet?"* Don't teach to it — let Wonder Wall questions, monthly units, Life of Fred, read-alouds, and travel projects do the work. This just shows you the gaps in time to weave them into daily life.

**Marking system:**
- `☐` Not yet
- `◐` In progress / introduced
- `✔` Mastered — add the date and one line of evidence ("counted the gift shop coins himself, 3/12")

Dated evidence notes double as portfolio entries for the annual evaluation.

**Reminder:** Florida does NOT require homeschoolers to follow these standards. The evaluation bar is progress *commensurate with his ability*. This tracker exists so nothing important slips through a curiosity-led year — not to create pressure.
